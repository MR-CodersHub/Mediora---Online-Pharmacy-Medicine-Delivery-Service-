Patient & Delivery Information

Valid Prescription Checklist

To ensure swift approval without delays, ensure your uploaded prescription contains:

Doctor Credentials

Doctor's full name, medical license / DEA number, clinic address, and handwritten or verifiable e-signature.

Patient Identity

Patient name and age/DOB matching the delivery order profile.

Medication & Dosage

Exact medicine name, strength (e.g. 500mg), dosage form, quantity, and daily frequency.

Valid Prescription Date

Written within the valid statutory timeframe (typically within 6 months for standard Rx, 30 days for controlled substances).

Help & FAQs

Prescription Ordering FAQs

Common questions regarding doctor slips, e-prescriptions, and insurance coverage.

Can I upload a picture taken from my smartphone?

Yes! Take a well-lit photo of your physical prescription slip with all 4 corners visible. Avoid heavy shadows or blurs. Both JPG and PNG smartphone photos are accepted.

How long does the pharmacist verification process take?

During standard hours, our clinical pharmacists review uploaded prescriptions within 10 to 15 minutes. Once approved, you will receive an SMS and email notification with an order confirmation link.

Do you accept insurance or Medicare for prescriptions?

Yes. Our pharmacy accepts most major insurance providers and Medicare Part D plans. After uploading your slip, our billing coordinator will calculate your exact copay amount before dispensing.

Can my doctor send the e-prescription directly to Mediora?

Certainly. Your prescribing doctor can route e-prescriptions directly to Mediora Dispensary (NCPDP ID: 994821) through any standard EHR software (Epic, Cerner, AthenaHealth).
