Clinical Health Tips

Essential Home Medicine Best Practices

Practical safety guidance curated by our dispensary pharmacists for your home.

Proper Temperature Control

Avoid storing medications in steam-filled bathrooms or on sunny window sills. Heat and moisture rapidly degrade active pharmacological compounds. Keep room temperature medicines below 25°C (77°F).

Child-Resistant Storage

Always keep prescription and OTC caplets in high, locked cabinets out of reach of curious toddlers. Never refer to medications as "candies" or "sweet treats" to persuade children to take them.

Safe Disposal of Expired Drugs

Do not flush unused antibiotics or painkillers down the toilet as they contaminate groundwater. Return expired blister packs to our pharmacy drop box for certified ecological medical disposal.
