Patient FAQ

Support & Order Policies

Answers to common inquiries regarding cancellations, delivery delays, and product returns.

Can I cancel or modify an order after placing it?

Orders can be modified or cancelled freely before our cleanroom pharmacist seals the medication package. Once a prescription order has received final pharmacist seal and entered the cold-chain dispatch courier stage, cancellation is restricted per pharmacy board safety laws.

What is your return policy for medicines and equipment?

Under federal regulations, opened prescription medicines cannot be returned or restocked once delivered. However, unopened OTC items, diagnostic devices, and sealed equipment can be returned within 30 days for a full refund or exchange.

What happens if my medicine arrives damaged or unsealed?

If your package shows signs of tampering, damaged seals, or broken temperature indicator strips, immediately refuse the handover or call our hotline. We dispatch a priority emergency replacement at zero additional charge within 60 minutes.

How do I track my delivery courier in real-time?

Once dispatched, you will receive an SMS containing a direct live tracking link showing the courier's GPS vehicle location and estimated delivery countdown.
