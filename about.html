Our Journey

From Community Pharmacy to Digital Healthcare Pioneer

Mediora was founded by a team of licensed clinical pharmacists and healthcare technology specialists who witnessed firsthand the struggles vulnerable patients faced when obtaining vital prescriptions. Lengthy dispensary queues, confusing drug substitution notices, and stock shortages were compromising health outcomes.

In response, we engineered a clinical workflow combining digital prescription ingestion with licensed cleanroom verification. Every order is reviewed by a PharmD specialist before being sealed in tamper-evident packaging and assigned to temperature-monitored couriers.

Cleanroom Protocols

Responsible Medicine Handling & Verification

Medicines are bioactive agents that require careful environmental control and strict audit trails. Our specialized facilities operate under stringent Good Dispensing Practice (GDP) standards.

Continuous Cold-Chain Monitoring

Insulin, biologics, and specialized antibiotics remain in calibrated 2°C to 8°C IoT-monitored refrigerators until dispatch in thermal insulated pouches.

Rigorous Expiry Date Auditing

Our inventory system enforces strict FIFO (First In, First Out) rules. No medicine with less than 6 months remaining shelf-life is ever shipped to patients.

Tamper-Evident Medical Packaging

Orders are sealed in numbered, tamper-evident security bags with peel-proof indicator tape, ensuring patient receiving safety.
