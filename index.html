Patient FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions regarding prescriptions, shipping timelines, or medicine authenticity? We’re here to help.

Do I need a prescription to order medicines from Mediora?

Medicines marked with the red Rx Required badge require a valid prescription written by a licensed medical practitioner. Over-the-counter products, vitamins, and healthcare devices can be purchased directly without a doctor’s slip.

How does prescription verification work?

When you submit a prescription through our Upload page, our licensed pharmacists inspect the doctor's registration number, patient name, prescribed medication dosage, and valid issue date. Once verified, your order status moves to dispensing.

How are temperature-sensitive medicines handled?

Refrigerated items such as insulin, biologics, and select liquid suspensions are packed in thermal-insulated medical boxes with medical-grade cold gel packs, maintaining temperatures between 2°C and 8°C during transit.

What are your delivery fees and delivery windows?

Orders over $35 enjoy complimentary free express delivery. Standard delivery is just $4.99. Orders within metro coverage areas qualify for 2-hour rush courier delivery.
